=hyperplane::sample_hyperplane=, =ohtst::optimize_hyperplane=, and =pafi::pafi_profile= now return =FluxError::InvalidConfig= when the engine thermostat has =gamma = 0=. That setting is NVE dynamics, which samples an energy shell started at the anchor and not the canonical ensemble, so the constrained mean force, OH-TST curvature probe, and PAFI barrier it gave were biased with no error reported. With =gamma > 0= the sampled mean force and the profile do not depend on the friction, the constraint scheme, or the thermostat temperature beyond the =k_B T= in the reference; the documentation now says so.
