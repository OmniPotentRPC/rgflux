=kappa::bennett_chandler::shoot_reactive_trajectories= and =shoot_bennett_chandler= now honor =KappaConfig::commit_checkpoints= via =ShootingConfig::kappa= (and =ShootingConfig::with_commit_checkpoints=), ending each half-trajectory once =commit_checkpoints= consecutive checkpoints agree on a committed side and copying that side into every later checkpoint of \(\kappa(t)\). =commit_checkpoints = 0= runs every half to =steps=, and =commit_checkpoints > steps= returns =FluxError::InvalidConfig=.
