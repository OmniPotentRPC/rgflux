=kappa::KappaConfig= gains an opt-in =early_commitment= flag (default =false=, setter =with_early_commitment=). When it is set, =kappa::bennett_chandler::shoot_reactive_trajectories= and =shoot_bennett_chandler= end each half-trajectory once =KappaConfig::commit_checkpoints= consecutive checkpoints agree on a committed side and copy that side into every later checkpoint of \(\kappa(t)\); =commit_checkpoints= must then not exceed =steps= or =FluxError::InvalidConfig= is returned. The configuration reaches the shooter through the new =ShootingConfig::kappa= field, and =ShootingConfig::with_commit_checkpoints= sets the window. With the flag off, which is the default, every half runs to =steps= and results are unchanged.
