Lammpc configuration updates and failed steps preserve the last accepted
Rust-side state. A failed step restores that state. The session is
recreated only when the step error is a lammpc backend error. Another
step error, including an unavailable backend, does not recreate the
session. Timesteps are compared relatively. =pair_style=, =pair_coeffs=,
=boundary= and =suffix= reject non-ASCII text, NULs, newlines, carriage
returns, and semicolons. =extra_setup= omits empty lines and lines made
only of spaces and tabs. A line of only a newline, carriage return,
vertical tab, or form feed is rejected. Every other line must be ASCII.
Leading spaces and tabs are kept, and the first word must be =neighbor=,
=neigh_modify=, =comm_modify= or =pair_modify=. Non-ASCII text, a NUL,
a newline, a carriage return, and a semicolon are rejected before the
forbidden-word scan. The words =if=, =partition=, =shell=, =jump=,
=python=, =label= and =next= are rejected when the command-text
check has passed, as is =include= except as the =neigh_modify=
keyword followed by a group id. When the scan finds no forbidden
word, quotes, =$=, =&=, =#=, and ASCII controls other than tab are
rejected. An engine whose session cannot be recreated after a lammpc
error is poisoned. Later fallible methods and force evaluation return
an error. Infallible getters return the last accepted state.
