=ohtst::optimize_hyperplane=, =ohtst::optimize_hyperplane_from_minimum=, =ohtst::relax_reactant=, =OhtstConfig=, =OhtstEstimate= and =ReactantMinimum= are removed. They held the seed normal fixed and returned \(\sqrt{\kappa/\mu}/(2\pi)\,\exp(-\Delta V/k_{\mathrm{B}}T)\) from the potential rise along that line and one sampled reactant curvature. That is a one-dimensional harmonic estimate and not the optimal hyperplanar rate of Jóhannesson and Jónsson, which maximizes the free energy of the plane. The =ohtst= module now holds that method.
