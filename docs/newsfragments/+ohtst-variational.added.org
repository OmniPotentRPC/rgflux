=ohtst= is optimal hyperplanar transition state theory, moved here from =rgsaddle=: =PlaneOptimizer= and =optimize_plane= climb the free energy of the dividing hyperplane in offset and orientation, =basin_density= and =reversible_work= give the two factors of the rate, =ohtst_rate= combines them, =transmission_coefficient= and =plane_transmission= give the recrossing factor, and =anharmonic_correction= and the thermodynamic integration samplers give the anharmonic terms. =adaptive_ohtst= places the sampling along a string of planes. Surfaces are =rgsaddle::PointSurface= values, and the functions return =rgsaddle::SaddleError=.
