PAFI integrates the plane means with the cubic Hermite rule and takes the barrier from the global maximum of that interpolant, including a maximum that is not next to the highest sample. Two planes reduce the rule to the trapezoid rule, three planes are exact for a quadratic gradient, and four or more planes are exact for a cubic gradient. =reactant_log_partition= is the log of the doubled-trapezoid reactant partition integral of =exp(-(F(s) - F(s_0)) / kT)= up to the dividing segment, and =normalized_log_rate= is =0.5 * ln(kT / (2 pi)) - Delta F / kT - reactant_log_partition=. =FreeEnergyProfile= also reports =dividing_arc=, =reactant_frequency= from the reactant Hessian at the first image, and =quadrature_residual=.
