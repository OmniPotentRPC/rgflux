PAFI integrates the plane means with the cubic Hermite rule and takes the barrier from the global maximum of that interpolant, including a maximum that is not next to the highest sample. On each segment that maximum is exact: the derivative is a cubic solved in closed form, and a vanishing leading coefficient uses the stable quadratic formula, including a zero linear coefficient. Two planes reduce the rule to the trapezoid rule, three planes are exact for a quadratic gradient, and four or more planes are exact for a cubic gradient. =Z_R= stays the doubled trapezoid of =exp(-F / kT)= up to the dividing segment, evaluated in log space, and the normalized rate is =sqrt(kT / 2π) exp(-ΔF / kT) / Z_R=. The exponential of =ln Z_R= and the Lagrange derivative weights stay in log space, and a non-finite exponential returns =NonFinite=. The Hessian step is a fraction of the displacement, so a translation does not change it. The first image is accepted only when its gradient is flat and its Hessian is positive definite off the zero modes. =FreeEnergyProfile= also reports =dividing_arc=, =reactant_frequency=, and =quadrature_residual=. The zero-temperature check remains the trapezoid comparison and keeps its default tolerance.
