=pafi::FreeEnergyProfile::reactant_log_partition= is the log of the doubled-trapezoid reactant partition =2 * integral of exp(-(F(s) - F(s_0)) / kT) ds= up to the dividing segment, formed in log space, and =normalized_log_rate= is =0.5 * ln(kT / (2 pi)) - Delta F / kT - reactant_log_partition=. The normalized rate follows the sampled reactant well and no longer takes the Gaussian partition =sqrt(2 pi kT) / omega_0=; =reactant_frequency= stays on the profile as a diagnostic. =pafi::pafi_profile= accepts a reactant image that is not stationary when the mass-weighted Hessian off the zero modes is positive definite, and certifies it with a dense eigensolver for up to 128 coordinates.
