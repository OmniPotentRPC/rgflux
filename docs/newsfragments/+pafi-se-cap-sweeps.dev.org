The PAFI rate standard-error cap now bounds only the lower tail of the Hermite barrier estimate, with a one sided normal quantile in place of the two sided one, so the cap meets its stated failure budget. The widening, rate, refinement and end-plane standard-error caps each have an opt-in seed sweep with fixed seeds, run with =cargo test -- --ignored sweep=, and the rgpot cap has one that also needs =--features rgpot=. Each sweep requires the observed failure rate to stay within the per-assertion budget. The end-plane sweep draws its two profiles from independent random streams, because the cap sums their variances and profiles that share a seed agree on every common plane. Tests check that the streams share no plane seed and that the trial differences have the variance the cap assumes.
