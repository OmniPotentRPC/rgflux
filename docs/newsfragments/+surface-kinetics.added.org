The path-based kinetics of =rgsaddle= live here now. =pafi::cartesian= is projected average force integration in the Cartesian metric over a =rgsaddle::PointSurface=, with the overdamped walk or a =HyperplaneSampler=, the projected-gradient estimator and trapezoid integration. =kappa::surface= is Bennett-Chandler shooting with uniform-mass BAOAB on a =PointSurface=. =defect_pair= is the defect-pair rate catalogue and =pair_network= the network built from it. The =pafi-repro= feature enables the reference replay test.
