# towncrier release notes start

* 0.2.1 (2026-11-22)

** Developer

- The end-plane standard-error cap sweep now draws its two trial profiles from
  independent random streams. The cap sums the two profile variances, so profiles
  that share a seed can agree on every common plane and understate the
  disagreement budget. Tests now verify that the two streams share no plane seed
  and that the trial differences have the variance the cap assumes.
- The native-engine integration tests now check constrained forces, kinetic temperature, PAFI barriers, and normalized rates across friction values and both RATTLE and SHAKE, including negative controls at an incorrect temperature.

** Changed

- =KappaConfig::default().commit_checkpoints= is =0=.
  =kappa::bennett_chandler::shoot_reactive_trajectories= and
  =shoot_bennett_chandler= integrate each half-trajectory for
  =ShootingConfig::steps=. Early commitment stays opt-in through
  =ShootingConfig::kappa= and requires a positive checkpoint count together
  with a strictly positive =commitment_distance=. Invalid criteria return a
  typed configuration error.

** Fixed

- =hyperplane::sample_hyperplane=, =ohtst::optimize_hyperplane=, and =pafi::pafi_profile= now return =FluxError::InvalidConfig= when the engine thermostat does not provide canonical sampling. White noise with =gamma = 0= is NVE dynamics, which samples an energy shell started at the anchor and not the canonical ensemble, so the constrained mean force, OH-TST curvature probe, and PAFI barrier it gave were biased with no error reported. A supported colored-noise thermostat remains canonical at =gamma = 0=. The OH-TST reactant curvature is that same canonical average, and =optimize_hyperplane= refuses NVE with the same error before it snapshots the engine or starts the reactant search. In a canonical regime the sampled mean force and the profile do not depend on the friction, the constraint scheme, or the thermostat temperature beyond the =k_B T= in the reference; the documentation now says so.
- =ohtst::optimize_hyperplane= and =ohtst::optimize_hyperplane_from_minimum= now compare the normal slope \(\lvert -\mathbf{n} \cdot \mathbf{F} \rvert\) at the seed plane against the physical force tolerance =OhtstConfig::reactant_fmax= instead of =stats::SERIES_FLAT_TOL=, so a seed plane placed on a relaxed saddle with force round-off keeps its offset directly.

* 0.2.0 (2026-11-21)

** Breaking

- =FreeEnergyProfile::reactant_partition=, =normalized_rate= and
  =normalized_rate_error= are =Option<f64>= instead of =f64=.
  =reactant_log_partition= and =normalized_log_rate= stay authoritative for every
  finite value, and a finite log stays on the profile.
  =reactant_partition= and =normalized_rate= are =None= when the exponential of
  that finite log overflows or underflows.
  =normalized_rate_error= holds =exp(normalized_log_rate + ln(sigma))=, where
  sigma is =barrier_error= divided by k_B T; it is =Some(0.0)= when sigma is zero
  and =None= only when that exponential overflows, underflows, or is not finite.
- =PafiConfig::zero_temperature_tol= is =Option<f64>= instead of =f64=.
  =None=, the default, scales the tolerance with =planes=.
  An explicit value is used at every plane count, including a value equal to the
  tolerance at the default plane count.

** Added

- =kappa::bennett_chandler::shoot_reactive_trajectories= and =shoot_bennett_chandler= support opt-in early commitment through =ShootingConfig::kappa=. Fixed-length shooting remains the default. Enabling the optimization requires an explicit positive checkpoint count and signed-distance threshold; invalid criteria return a typed configuration error.
- =OhtstConfig::reactant_fmax= sets the largest force component accepted at the OH-TST reactant minimum, default =1e-6=, so a reactant relaxed to the rounding floor of its forces gives a rate instead of stalling. The reactant is relaxed with the =rgmin= L-BFGS solver, and a relaxation that ends above the tolerance returns =FluxError::ReactantNotRelaxed=. =ohtst::relax_reactant= relaxes a reactant once, and =ohtst::optimize_hyperplane_from_minimum= evaluates an exit from a supplied =ReactantMinimum= after checking its force against the tolerance.

** Developer

- The PAFI rate standard-error cap now bounds only the lower tail of the
  Hermite barrier estimate, with a one sided normal quantile in place of the
  two sided one, so the cap meets its stated failure budget. The standard-error
  caps have opt-in fixed-seed sweeps that require the observed failure rate to
  stay within the per-assertion budget. Run
  =cargo test --features rgpot -- --ignored sweep= to execute all six: five
  native sweep tests and one rgpot sweep test.

** Fixed

- Hermite mean-force integration now rejects non-finite gradients with =FluxError::NonFinite=, matching =SimpsonIntegral= and preventing NaN or infinity values from producing a successful cumulative profile.
- PAFI integrates the plane means with the cubic Hermite rule and takes the barrier from the global maximum of that interpolant, including a maximum that is not next to the highest sample. Two planes reduce the rule to the trapezoid rule, three planes are exact for a quadratic gradient, and four or more planes are exact for a cubic gradient. =reactant_log_partition= and =normalized_log_rate= stay the log of the reactant partition and of the normalized rate, now =ln(sqrt(2 π kT) / ω_0)= and =ln(ω_0) - ln(2π) - ΔF / kT= from the reactant Hessian at the first image. =FreeEnergyProfile= also reports =dividing_arc=, =reactant_frequency=, and =quadrature_residual=. The zero-temperature check remains the trapezoid comparison and keeps its default tolerance.
- Report the PAFI truncation instead of a silent zero. =standard_error= and =barrier_error= combine the batch-means plane-force error with the absolute difference between the cubic Hermite integral and the trapezoid rule on the same nodes. =MeanForceIntegral= is the seam for another deterministic rule; =SimpsonIntegral= is composite Simpson and rejects an even plane count rather than falling back. There is no in-crate Gaussian process.
- The free-energy =standard_error= and =barrier_error= are a scaled
  Euclidean norm of the weighted plane uncertainties. A finite uncertainty
  whose square overflows stays finite.

* 0.1.0 (2026-11-19)

** Added

- =ffs::forward_flux_rate= computes direct Forward Flux Sampling transition rates across ordered reaction-coordinate interfaces over any =DynamicsEngine=, with batch-means initial escape flux uncertainty, cluster-variance conditional stage probabilities, and engine state restoration.
- =hyperplane::sample_hyperplane= samples the mean holonomic constraint force and retained configurations of a plane, and =ohtst::optimize_hyperplane= places the offset of a fixed-normal plane on its stationary surface and returns the harmonic hyperplanar TST rate with a delta-method standard error.
- =pafi::pafi_profile= and =pafi::pafi_profile_dyn= turn the images of a converged reaction path into a free-energy profile, barrier, dividing plane, and normalized rate with propagated standard errors. They sample hyperplanes orthogonal to a mass-metric spline through the images, check the path force against the zero-temperature energy change, add rounds of sampling until an optional target error is reached, and restore the engine on return.
- Added an optional =lammpc= feature with =engine::lammpc::LammpcEngine=, a direct =DynamicsEngine= backend over =lammpc_session_*= for LAMMPS-driven trajectories.
- Initial =rgflux= crate scaffold: the =DynamicsEngine= trait and native BAOAB/RATTLE reference backend, feature-gated =rgpot= static PES and =rgpot-session= MD session (=openmm=, =nblib=, =lammps=) adapters, batch-means and stationarity diagnostics in =stats=, the Bennett-Chandler reactive-flux estimator in =kappa::bennett_chandler=, and the =network= module layout.

** Developer

- The curved PAFI integration test now compares the last free-energy increment with the one from a path that continues past it, so natural spline endpoints fail the test. It runs 9 planes with the default time step and the default zero-temperature tolerance. =cargo test= builds with =opt-level = 2= through =[profile.test]=, and the curved test takes 0.7 s instead of 44 s.

** Changed

- Depend on the crates.io releases of =rgsaddle= 0.5.0, =rgmin= 0.6.0, and =rgpot-core= 3.7.0 with caret requirements, so downstream builds can unify patch releases. Only the =lammpc= feature enables the =rgpot-core= =profile= and =schema= features, so =rgpot= and =rgpot-session= build without the =capnp= CLI.
- The value stored by default in =PafiConfig::zero_temperature_tol= is now about 2.6% instead of 1%. The zero-temperature check compares the path-force integral discrepancy with this dimensionless tolerance after dividing by the larger of one engine energy unit and the endpoint energy change magnitude. While the field retains its default value, the effective tolerance scales as =2 (pi^2 / 12) / (planes - 1)^2=; an explicitly different value is used unchanged.

** Fixed

- =ffs::forward_flux_rate= restores positions, velocities, the periodic cell, the thermostat, and the holonomic constraint on return, and leaves the stochastic stream where the flux and trial reseeds put it. =ohtst::optimize_hyperplane= takes the flux mass from =rgsaddle::flux_mass=, keeps a length mismatch on =FluxError::Shape=, and keeps a non-positive mass on =FluxError::InvalidConfig=.
- =stats::batch_means= returns the first sample as the mean of a series that is constant up to rounding, so identical non-dyadic samples are no longer averaged to a neighbouring float.
- Compute the PAFI reactant partition in log space with shifted log-sum-exp trapezoid quadrature so deep free-energy wells no longer overflow the partition integral. Propagate =ln Z_R= through normalized rate evaluation and uncertainty propagation while retaining legacy linear-rate and partition fields for compatibility.
- Correct PAFI mass-metric projection, spline geometry, adaptive sampling, profile integration, uncertainty propagation, and engine-state restoration.
