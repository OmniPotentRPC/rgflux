* Engine-agnostic kinetics

Saddle-point searches in =rgsaddle= and local or Riemannian minimizers in =rgmin= query static energy and gradient surfaces @@rst::cite:`goswamiEnhancedClimbingImage2026,goswamiEfficientExploration2025`@@. Finite-temperature kinetics additionally requires finite-timestep propagation, thermal velocity seeding, dividing-surface velocity boundary rules, and holonomic hyperplane constraints.

=rgflux::engine::DynamicsEngine= captures that contract in an object-safe trait. Samplers such as Bennett-Chandler shooting @@rst::cite:`bennettMolecularDynamics1977,chandlerStatisticalMechanics1978`@@, PAFI @@rst::cite:`swinburneUnsupervisedCalculation2018`@@, OH-TST @@rst::cite:`johannessonOptimizationHyperplanar2001,bligaardOptimizationHyperplanar2005`@@, and Forward Flux Sampling @@rst::cite:`allenSimulatingRareEvents2006,allenForwardFluxEfficiency2006`@@ drive any backend implementing =DynamicsEngine= without coupling rate estimators to a particular MD package.

=engine::native= provides an in-process BAOAB Langevin integrator @@rst::cite:`leimkuhlerRationalConstruction2012,leimkuhlerGeodesicIntegration2016`@@ with SHAKE @@rst::cite:`ryckaertShake1977`@@ and RATTLE @@rst::cite:`andersenRattle1983`@@ hyperplane projection over any =rgsaddle::PointSurface=, =eindir_core::DifferentiableObjective=, or =rgpot= callback surface. A direct =engine::lammpc= backend (feature =lammpc=) drives LAMMPS through =lammpc_session_*= with Cap'n Proto =PotentialConfig= and =ForceInput= carriers from =rgpot-core=. External molecular dynamics engines also enter through =rgpot-session=, wrapping =rgpot='s engine-agnostic MD session ABI.
